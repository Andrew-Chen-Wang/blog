---
title: "Taro Soup Sweet"
description: "Big taro for sweet soup (small taro is sticky and better for salty soups with meats and vegetables)"
pubDate: 2025-10-24
---

Big taro for sweet soup (small taro is sticky and better for salty soups with meats and vegetables)  
Chop into small pieces  
Boil sago, turn off heat and let it steam when done  
Boil yu tou until ready, powdery texture but still like small chunks  
Add sago to yu tou when done  
Optional, da sui or boil until very powdery  
Then add coconut and milk and sugar
