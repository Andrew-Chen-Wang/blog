This is like 8 servings/drinking cups

1.5 cups of white rice  
2 tablespoons of sugar  
1 Large cinnamon stick (or 2 small ones), broken up  
1-3 teaspoons of vanilla  
12 ounce can evaporated milk  
12 ounce can sweetened condensed milk  
4 cups hot water  
2-4 cups of milk (or 2-4 cups of water; it should be 4 cups in total of liquid, so you could have 2 cups of milk and 2 cups of water)

1. Boil 4 cups of water
2. Wash rice. Place in container/bowl. Add 4 cups of hot water. Break up cinnamon stick(s) and place in. Cover with plastic wrap. Place in fridge. Leave overnight or wait at least 4 hours.
3. (Optional) You can remove most of the cinnamon stick(s)
4. Blend the rice in separate batches. Puree until it’s very smooth and forms a watery paste like texture. About 3-4 minutes of blending.
5. Use a fine strainer or cheese cloth. Pour the blended mixture over a large pitcher. Strain out as much liquid as possible, pushing on the solids with a spatula or spoon
6. Repeat for the rest
7. Stir in the condensed milk, evaporated milk, and the regular milk/water combination.
8. Taste and add sugar or water if needed.
9. Chill and add ice.
