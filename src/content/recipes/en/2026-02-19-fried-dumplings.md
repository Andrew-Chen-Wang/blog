---

- Get a pot and turn on the heat
- As it’s warming up, fill the surface with frozen dumplings
- Then unleash a ton of oil and shake pot horizontally
- Get a little less than a metric cup of water and top it with some flour, like 2 tbsp worth. Less is fine, just don’t add too much
- Then just let it fry

A method Rosa and I saw online
