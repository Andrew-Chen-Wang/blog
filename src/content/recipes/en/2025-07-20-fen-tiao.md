Hong shu fen tiao  
Da guo. Put water and boil not too big boil.  
Put noodles in and cut it a little  
Boil soft. Take out noodles.  
Cao ground pork with Doubanjiang  
Add the noodles once pork is done. Add soy sauce.  
Stir/mix.  
Add more soy sauce if needed.
