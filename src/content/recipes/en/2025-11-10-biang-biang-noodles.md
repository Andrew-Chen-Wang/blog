So many videos, yet this white guy is pretty good

400g flour  
8g salt  
200g water

Make into a ball, cover with bowl  
Wait 30 minutes  
Knead ferociously  
Test for gluten window by stretching so that it doesn’t break  
Shape dough into rectangle: 6 inches long, 12 inches wide. Square up or cut edges. Then cut into 6 pieces, 2 in wide. Each noodle sheet is 100 grams.

To store, brush with neutral oil and stack them by domino leaning them, then let is sit up to 3 days, waiting at least a few hours can prevent breaking when stretching
