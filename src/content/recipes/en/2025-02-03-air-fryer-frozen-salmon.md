---
title: "Air Fryer Frozen Salmon"
description: "Frozen salmon on aluminum foil, 375 F air fry"
pubDate: 2025-02-03
updatedDate: 2025-04-30
---

- Cook rice
- Frozen salmon on aluminum foil, 375 F air fry
- Slather oil and some salmon rub
- Air fry for 18-19 minutes. At 6-8 minute mark, use some chop sticks to poke some holes in it to cook center
- Optional cook a runny egg to mix the yolk with the rice and add some soy sauce and green onions
