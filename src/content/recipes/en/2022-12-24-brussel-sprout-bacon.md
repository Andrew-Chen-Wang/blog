---
title: "Brussel Sprout Bacon"
description: "Chop up some bacon bits"
pubDate: 2022-12-24
---

- Chop up some bacon bits
- Halve brussel sprouts
- Chop up a red chili
- Dump in a bowl with vegetable oil
- Cook until smoke appears and put back in bowl
- Cook bacon in a little bit of oil until cooked through
- Set aside but don’t dump oil. Keep in pan.
- Cook brussel sprouts (if you want healthier, add a little water; it’ll also cook faster. Otherwise just use the oil from the bacon and fat)
- Add chopped garlic
- Add some sweet sauce (澳味甜面酱), oyster sauce, some of the chopped cooked chili, sesame oil, and sugar
- Add back bacon and cook together
