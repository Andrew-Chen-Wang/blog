Any chicken wash, cut into cubes, let it dry as much as possible  
Put some oil in pan  
Ginger a little bit  
Cook chicken for a bit  
Add some liao jiu for a bit  
Add sugar soy sauce and some water and boil. Add a bit more water not too much  
When chicken can be punctured easily very soft, chestnuts washed and boiled for 5-10 minutes.  
Water should mostly be gone  
Add oyster sauce
