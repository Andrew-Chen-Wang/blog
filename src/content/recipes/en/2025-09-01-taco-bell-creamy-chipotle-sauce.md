- 1/4 C mayo
- 1/4 C sour cream
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp onion powder
- 1/4 tsp chili powder
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tbsp pickled jalapeno juice
- 1 tbsp pickled jalapenos
This is basically it. You can make your own variations such as chopping the jalapeños to your preferred consistency, or adding more/less if you like. When I make it, I put everything in the food processor and let it do the chopping/mixing.  
I also highly recommend letting it sit in the fridge for at least a couple hours so the spices all have time to bloom.l
