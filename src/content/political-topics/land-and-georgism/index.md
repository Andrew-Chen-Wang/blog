Land, Georgism, landlording (a la pied a terre tax in NYC), etc. I talk about how in combination
with the deployment of capital from highly capitalized firms, you can see distortions in the market
from greed in growth and the individual. E.g. Luckin Coffee or Starbucks in Manhattan, to
an extent Dollar General squeezing out grocers in food deserts. The key for the NYC restaurants is
their ability to squeeze out local competition through being better guarantors or even potentially
being a higher bidder of rent as large multinational corporations compared to local food. This has
led to the corporatization of both Williamsburg and, of course, Manhattan.
