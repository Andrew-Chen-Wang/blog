Concentration is the thread running through most of what I
write. Private markets are the right default, but past a
certain size, capital stops competing and starts distorting:
private equity rolling up mom-and-pop businesses, undercutting
until the small competitor is gone, and platforms that resist
change because the status quo pays them. See
[Power Imbalance and Leverage from Money's Perspective](/blog/2026-05-04-what-business-is-too-powerful/),
[Tackling Monopolies](/blog/2024-08-15-solving-efficiency-against-monopolies/), and
[Most people never learn enough to deserve opinion](/blog/2026-08-02-most-people-never-learn-enough-to-deserve-an-opinion/).

I'm also interested in the effects of incentivization in the political and business aspect. Inspecting
how you can rally people for your cause or cause certain incentives like at Google which ends up with
toxic political work culture or VC ecosystem causing enshittification in the software market and even
NYC restaurant market.
