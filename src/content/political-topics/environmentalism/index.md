Environmentalism, particularly protection of nature, the nuance of environmentalism and
human productivity (and solutions to make both work out), the Great Lakes, forests, and national
parks. I'm not a big hiker, but I appreciate being in nature, being surrounded by animals, letting
animals bask in private, and having the privacy through being in forests / being connected to
nature. Funny story: my dad enjoys the suburbs, and I have more and more friends love suburban
living. My dad grew up on a tiny farm in China, and moved to the city in his adulthood. He hated
the city, not just because of the sectionalism in China (that persists even today regarding white
skin vs. tan skin which is the opposite of America), but because he is kind of a walking faux pas
city-wise. There is a dichotomy between urbanists who are usually political and just casual people
who want the space, tranquility, low cost of living, low stress, and low pollution of a suburb.
