The power of the individual: my favorite entertainment are Dune, both cinematic and book series,
and Andor. I do think people need to struggle, but not suffer. I've written about this in a blog.
However, what I haven't written about but is widely discussed is Dune's thinking on the power of
an individual. Not just in the struggle, but politically as well with the Messiah figure, using
the Voice in real life (metaphorically), etc. But religion will always be a thing. It won't ever
die, and I think tolerance of differences should be learned.
