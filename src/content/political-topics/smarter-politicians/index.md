Politicians should be smarter: I think the modern politician is far more representative in the U.S.
compared to most countries because the representatives are actual residents of the districts or
states they represent. However, the original intent for politicians in America was for them to be
educated. There is an intellectual elitism in the American left that believes college educated
individuals would naturally be more leftist and that their policies are better, but simply having
that degree does not make them more knowledgeable on political issues and solutions. Because of
who is now entering politics, which over the last 80 years has been far more charismatic than
earlier American politicians, I think the overall intellectualism, particularly in political
theory, but also avoidance of populism has decreased. My favorite modern politician is Barack
Obama. I've found interviews of him talking about the rise of China and him being anti-woke and
purity tests dating back to 2019. He's made mistakes that I've criticized on online forums (tl;dr
some of them: foreign (in)action in the Middle East, particularly the Arab Spring, starting
to write flurries of executive orders that Trump continued in his first term and went wild in his
second). But he was the best politician because he was practical while remaining ideological.
Politics is slow moving, and people never appreciate that. It's why I say being a president is
just a job. It's an honor to be one, but it's just a job. Or at least it's supposed to be with
minimal corruption:/ Additionally, Obama staffed people who understood the nuance, but Mayor
Mamdani is completing tasks that make him charismatic and to the Democratic base successful
when his departments are also executing policies that pushes NYC's problems down the road
like rent control. I still think Mamdani is a good, charismatic politician, but he chose
people who are too ideological. President Trump has the same problem, though he doesn't mind it
as much (probably because he doesn't care about America's long term future and is devoid of
political theory and Democratic ideals, though I do believe in Susie Wiles when she says Trump
is just trolling the left that he'll stay for a third term). President Trump's problem is that

The Christian faction was shunned out by President Reagan (and overseas, by Lee Kwan Yew), but
