There's a joke that when women want to talk, they seek to have a listening partner; instead, when women talk with
men, men jump into giving a solution. I think politics, wannabe politicians, and general discourse tend to jump
to providing solutions without listening to an entire problem.

When people hear a problem revolving politics, they just want to give a solution. From online discourse, they tend to
be more extreme or ideological given those people are the most likely to comment or standout in social media
algorithms. Solutions have merit, but they tend to address a problem head on without understand their drawbacks. Because
people believe they understand a problem without much context, they propose a solution that directly addresses the
high level overview of the problem. Upon criticism, one might be able to counter on the spot, but it's obvious that
it's not thought through. Dug deeper into a solution, and it's obvious there's no understanding of the full picture
and minute details of a political problem. And take a solution one step further, inspecting their long-term consequences and cascading effects, and problems' difficulties become a lot more nuanced. For example, imposing tariffs may have been done by Trump, but Biden continued them; Obama started leaned more into executive orders, and Trump went into overdrive. Precedence is hard to revert. I've been saying this for years: why is post-encouragement discouragement futile? It's because precedence affects culture and mindset, and, at scale, that is hard to break because the precedence becomes justification for a small proportion of people that eventually grows. (On the flip side, encouragement or, individually, forming habits is also hard. It takes a little bit of consistency and agreement to encourage, but I think discouragement is even harder).

Of course, folks want to solve problems, and online discourse tends to reward the people who do the least research and short form content rather than long form discussion. I instead implore that those same people introspect their proposals and addressed problem to find all the trade-offs and criticism beforehand and why people are rightfully against solutions. Don't be the person that bluntly justifies that critics against your solution are amoral or uneducated.
