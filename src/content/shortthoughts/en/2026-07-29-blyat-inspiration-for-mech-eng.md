The United States needs more engineers, more talented engineers, to ensure knowledge of how to build things
remain in the U.S. What powers economies is what we produce, and to leave behind everything from farming
to manufacturing we leave behind a huge potential for economic growth... but also creativity.

May I introduce Blyat: https://space.bilibili.com/6989655/?spm_id_from=333.788.upinfo.detail.click

This guy builds the funniest, most extreme guns (that don't shoot but violently recoil) and vehicles (like a moving tank or an out of control race car) ever in China.

Maybe we should allow kids to take over a warehouse like Mr. Beast did with his crew and just have fun building
things. Possibly, it requires an owner who is an engineer to field ideas that can be tested on the grounds.
Then invite some teens over and show them the cool stuff and post it on YouTube.
