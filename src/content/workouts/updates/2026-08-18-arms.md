2026-08-18 update on arms: this is embarrassing, but the average male arm size is 13 inches, and
it isn't until a year of working out now that I have a non-flexed arm of almost (off by a tick) 13
inches circumference. I've recently been cutting from 162.5lb to now 155lb over the last two weeks.
Suffice it to say, I self deprecatingly called myself a stick throughout my childhood. I seem to have a
very good metabolism, particularly from carbs (Wuhan genes? Though my mom is actually mostly Sichuanese
even though so many dishes I ate growing up were Wuhan stuff. I digress) Now I know, based on this
latest measurement, that I was truly a twig. I thought all my free manual labor for my dad
putting up drywalls, wooden planks, and painting and scrubbing would build muscle. Did jack. I bet
carrying a baby for a year gets parents more muscle than lifting weights. Another thing that is
slightly embarrassing is that I could do 30 underhand pull ups in middle school, but I was,
again, a stick with lots of soccer and free labor behind my belt. Now I have visceral fat apparently.
I've been trying to do overhand and neutral grip pull ups the last week, and I've barely gotten
past 2 on the overhand (on a push day) and 10 on the neutral (if I skipped all other exercises).
I don't think I have that much (hidden since I'm east asian) fat, but there's definitely something
there. We looked back in my high school photos, and I had abs (still a stick!). What happened to those.
Upon reflecting on my apparent achievement of being average now, do all men go to the gym? Or is
that 13 inches also including folks with lots of fat in their arms, but to what degree would that
skew the average? What manual labor did people do that I'm not aware of. Even from a sports
perspective, there wasn't weight training. For me playing soccer growing up, it was intense cardio
and skin cancer. But for 13.4 inches being the ages 20-29 average, are people going to the gym?
The majority of people don't go to the gym. It's also possibly my arm measuring is imprecise.
I could also just be losing fat. Man I wish I did like a dexa scan to see before and now. Would've
been cool to scientifically see it or add my stat to someone's scientific studies. I also
